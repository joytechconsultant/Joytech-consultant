About JCGSL

Bridging Risk Reduction With Peak Performance

JOYTECH CONSULTANT AND GENERAL SUPPLIES LTD is a registered firm of experts and consultants based in Nairobi, Kenya. Founded in 2021, we are a dynamic team driven by passion and expertise to assist organizations in mitigating operational risks while maximizing efficiency.

We work closely with client organizations locally and regionally to establish, develop, implement, and maintain international management systems, conduct strategic research, and supply quality corporate resources.

Our Comprehensive Portfolio

Integrated Consultancy & Supply Solutions

We deliver tailored solutions across three distinct operational pillars designed to strengthen organizational infrastructure.

Pillar A: ISO Standards & Management Consultancy

Quality Management Systems (QMS)

Development, training, implementation, and auditing based on the ISO 9001:2015 standard to optimize workflow efficiency.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Audits

Environmental Management (EMS)

Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA), Strategic Environmental Assessments (SEA), ISO 14001, and EMAS audits.

ISO 14000 EIA / SEA EMAS

Occupational Health & Safety (OHS)

OHSAS 18000, ISO 45001, BS8800, hazard identification, and workplace risk assessments for absolute safety compliance.

ISO 45001 OHSAS 18000 NOSA

Information Security & Records

ISO 27001 (ISMS) security controls, ISO 15489 records management, statistical analysis, and Integrated Management Information Systems.

ISO 27000 ISO 15489 IT Security

Food & Product Safety

HACCP principles, ISO/FSSC 22000, BRC, GLOBALGAP protocols, Good Manufacturing (GMP), and Hygiene Practices (GHP).

ISO 22000 HACCP GLOBALGAP

Risk Management & Forest Stewardship

ISO 31000 risk framework implementation, Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) certifications, Chain of Custody (CoC), and social responsibility.

ISO 31000 FSC / CoC Social Audits

Pillar B: Surveys & Organizational Research

Satisfaction & Work Environment

Employee engagement, customer satisfaction index, and work environment quality assessments designed for data-driven decisions.

Institutional Integrity Surveys

Corruption incidence and perception surveys, access to information evaluations, and service delivery improvement systems.

Social Mainstreaming & Health

Gender mainstreaming, disability mainstreaming, and workplace alcohol, drug & substance abuse prevalence surveys.

Pillar C: General Supplies & Contracting

Office Stationeries & Uniforms

ICT Maintenance & Hardware

Supply, repair, and ongoing servicing of enterprise servers, computers, laptops, printers, scanners, and peripheral equipment.

Building & Construction Materials

General Contracting & Renovations
